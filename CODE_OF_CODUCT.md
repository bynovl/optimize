# Code of Conduct

We're committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and supportive community. All participants are expected to uphold the following principles:

## Community Principles

- **Inclusivity:** Everyone is welcome. We encourage participation from people of all backgrounds, identities, and skill levels.
- **Respect:** Treat others with respect. Listen, be patient, and value different perspectives.
- **Kindness:** Be friendly and supportive. Offer help, encouragement, and constructive feedback.
- **Helpfulness:** If you see someone struggling or have knowledge to share, lend a hand. We grow stronger together.

## Expected Behavior

- Be welcoming and open-minded.
- Communicate clearly and kindly.
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, or exclusionary behavior.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Focus on helping, learning, and improving together.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, or threats.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Dismissing or belittling others’ contributions.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the maintainers or email os@bynovl.com. All reports will be handled confidentially and respectfully.

## Commitment

By participating in the Optimize community, you agree to uphold these values and help create a positive, inclusive environment for everyone.

Thank you in advncne for making this a welcoming and supportive space!
