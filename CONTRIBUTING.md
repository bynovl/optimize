# Contributing to Optimize 

Our goal is to create an inclusive, respectful, and supportive space where all voices are valued and everyone can contribute, regardless of background or experience. We welcome everyone to participate! 

## How you can contribute

- Join a [Discussion](https://github.com/bynovl/optimize/discussions) meant for technical and non-technical people. 
- Share your ideas, ask questions, and help others.
- Report bugs, suggest features, and participate in polls.
- Celebrate community achievements and give kudos to helpful members.

Want to contribute to code? Say hello contributors@bynovl.com. We're looking for a few dedicated folks passionate about helping businesses have a fighting chance in generative and traditional search.

## Guidelines
Before contributing make sure to read the full [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). The short version:

- Be welcoming and open-minded.
- Communicate clearly and kindly.
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination or exclusionary behavior.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Focus on helping, learning, and improving together.

## Need Help?

If you’re new or unsure, just ask! Our community is here to support you. A great place to start is heading to the [Community Support](https://github.com/bynovl/optimize/discussions).

No question is too basic, and every contribution matters.
